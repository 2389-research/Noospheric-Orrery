### Current Product Use and Implementation

- Discussion on the effectiveness of the shop app and its universal cart features
    - Participants mentioned that most people don’t know what the shop app is
    - Compared to Stripe’s link and PayPal
- Shopify’s attempt at a universal app for discovery and easy checkout
    - Unclear if users need to opt-in or opt-out
    - Mentioned that it may miss a universe of things not bought frequently from Shopify

### Challenges and Opportunities

- Potential reasons for the shop app not being extremely successful
    - Possible implementation flaws
    - Limited to Shopify brands, reducing utility
    - Lack of compelling and interesting experience
- Mentioned social streaming shopping experiences in Asia as an example of engaging shopping experiences

### Potential Solutions and Direction

- Alex suggested a chat interface for shopping
    - Natural and common form of communication (e.g., SMS, email)
    - Inspired by OpenAI’s concept of an AI agent performing multistep functions
    - Could log into favorite shopping sites and understand past orders for personalized shopping
    - Example: “I need 2 more cases of Celsius” and it handles the purchase
- Emphasis on leveraging LLMs for multistep workflows to provide a personalized and efficient shopping experience
    - Could handle tasks like finding the best products based on reviews and data

### Technical Considerations

- Discussion on the costs associated with LLM turns
    - Mentioned making the experience good first, then finding ways to make it cheaper
    - Potential for fine-tuning to reduce costs
- Mentioned the importance of intent in user interactions
    - Higher conversion rates when users show clear intent
    - Affiliate programs could be leveraged for monetization

- Agreement to continue discussions and explore the chat interface idea further
