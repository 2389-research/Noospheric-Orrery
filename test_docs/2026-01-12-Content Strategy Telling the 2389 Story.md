**What We're Building**

A lightweight content engine that turns our Wed/Fri shipping rhythm into public storytelling - so people know we're smart, building cool stuff, and want to follow along.

**The Goal**

Build a community around our skills (ie frontend) and products (ie Jeff). Get feedback. Attract collaborators. Discover which tool might become "the one" we focus on.

Things we can play aroudn with



The Pieces

1. Landing Pages (foundation)
- Every skill gets its own page with its story
- Structure: The problem/why we built it → How it works (in action) → CTA
- Some get video demos - specifically when the skill is complex, significant, or has visual payoff
- Videos embedded on the page (not linked out to YouTube)


2. Email Updates
- Every launch goes to our 300 subscribers
- Format: One personal sentence ("We kept running into X, so we built this") + 2-3 sentence teaser + link to
landing page
- Cadence: Up to 2x/week matching our Wed/Fri launches


3. X/Twitter
- Amplification channel - [[Harper Reed|Harper]] has a following there
- Can post video walkthroughs natively
- Exact approach (Harper's account vs. team account vs. both) TBD

4. Site Refresh (plugins.2389.dev)
- Add brief intro at top: who we are, what we're doing, why
- Add "Latest" section showing recent 2-3 launches
- Each skill links to its landing page
- Prominent mailing list signup
- Catalog stays as reference for browsing everything

**The Workflow**

5. Builder ships something
6. Builder does a 5-min brain dump (call or voice memo) with the writer
7. Writer creates landing page
8. Writer sends email to list
9. Post to X (format TBD)

Who Does What

- Builders: Ship things, give 5-min brain dump
- Sophie: Owns all landing pages and emails, keeps voice consistent
- Video: Figured out per-skill based on whether it's complex/significant/visual

Why This Works

- Sustainable: Builders just do a quick brain dump, one person handles the content
- Consistent: Same voice across all pages and emails
- PBuilds toward focus: By sharing publicly, we see what resonates - feedback tells us what's valuable, what people
would pay for
