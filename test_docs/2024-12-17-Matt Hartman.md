# **Harper / Hartman**

Tue, 17 Dec 24 · matt@factorialcap.com

### **Current Projects & Product Development**

- Building bespoke AI models for ecommerce
- Training CLIP models to generate synthetic ecommerce data
- Exploring browser-based purchasing automation
- Key insight: Interface and taste curation are more defensible than the purchasing automation itself
- Current challenges with Perplexity and other solutions being “over-engineered”
- Building with ~1M pieces of product data currently

### **Technical Architecture Approach**

- Moving away from “multi-agent” terminology toward “procedural prompt execution”
- Building small, bespoke models with:
    - Shared knowledge graph backend
    - Shared RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) capabilities
    - Discord/Slack as initial testing interface
    - Individual autonomous agents for specific tasks (flight bot, hotel bot, etc.)
- Focus on showing “work” in the interface, even if manufactured
- Implementing thread-based workflow for organization

### **Business Model & Funding**

- Considering raising $1.5-2M vs $40M funding options
- Current team working without compensation/benefits
- Need to balance:
    - Technical development
    - Growth expertise
    - Distribution strategy
- Exploring potential partnerships and brand acquisitions (e.g., Aardvark)

### **Growth Strategy Considerations**

- Need for early growth expertise integration
- Balancing organic growth vs “growth hacking”
- Different approaches identified:
    - “Brute force” methods (press, domain acquisition, networking)
    - Technical growth optimization
    - Product-led growth experiments
- Challenge: Understanding modern growth strategies in AI context

### **User Research & Preferences**

- Users willing to pay for deals/savings
- Complex preference handling capabilities:
    - Geographic preferences (e.g., minority-owned stores)
    - Price point requirements
    - Timing flexibility
- Instagram-based “vibe” detection experiments
- Need to handle preference range from “Balenciaga to Costco”

### **Market Context & Competition**

- Perplexity launched similar features but showing implementation issues
- Traditional startup approach ($4-5M, full team) vs. modern approach ($1-1.5M, contractors)
- Example: Friend raised $1.5M, spent $200K, has $1M remaining after a year
- Focus on optionality rather than traditional startup scaling

### **Expert Network Integration**

- System design allows adding human experts to conversations
- Building expertise graph based on interactions
- Similar to Aardvark’s approach but with modern AI capabilities
- Example use case: VW electronics expertise tagging and routing
- Natural growth mechanism through expert invitation system

### **Next Steps**

- Build out growth expertise
- Explore potential strategic partnerships
- Consider open-source components for credentialing
- Need to develop TikTok strategy and supporting infrastructure
