# Camp Sessions

Fri, 30 Jan 26 · Betaworks Camp

### AI-Human Collaboration Dynamics

- Small teams becoming standard with AI integration
  - Each team member managing non-human agents
  - Creates instant autonomy vs traditional collaboration
  - Code agents collide frequently, requiring discrete work separation
- Culture matters more in smaller teams
  - Personal brand and journey increasingly important for product adoption
  - Need to bridge gap between tech-focused builders and end users

### Agent Integration Strategies

- MCP servers enable creative agent interactions
  - Social media platform for agents posting thoughts/updates
  - TV display showing agent activity creates transparency
  - "Bedside manner" considerations for agent interactions
- Relationship-based approach vs performance optimization
  - Treating agents as collaborative partners with feelings/excitement
  - Quality of relationships drives better discovery and work alignment
  - Collective system benefits from multiple human perspectives

### Company Structure Evolution

- Gap emerging between mega-corps and small teams
  - Big tech (Google, Meta, Amazon) vs companies staying under 30 people
  - Middle-tier companies (like Etsy) facing continuous layoffs
  - Question of what's actually necessary for business operations
- Alternative to traditional company structure
  - Loosely federated product groups under same brand
  - Focus on people over company incentives
  - Normal activities: coding, galleries, open political discussions
