### Revenue Strategy & Market Positioning

- Company has significant development speed advantage (“time machine”) vs competitors
    - Can build products in days/weekends vs months for others
    - Market inefficiency window closing in ~6 months
- Revenue target: $100k/month ($1-1.2M annually) for sustainability
    - Flexibility decreases each month starting December
    - Need to move toward charging vs continued free development
- Three potential revenue paths:
    - High-value contracting ($100k projects) - test execution ability
    - Multiple small products ($5k/month each, need 20)
    - Social search + agents product (original pitch)

### Japan Market Opportunity

- Identified as potential “time machine” market with less AI competition
- Line app integration possibilities for social search/agents
- Challenges: No Japanese language skills, no local growth expertise
    - Solutions: Hire local growth person, minimal marketing budget ($5-10k)
- Could apply “rocket internet” model - rapid prototype successful US concepts

### Product Development & Launch Plans

- Blackboard launch tomorrow (Tuesday for better open rates)
    - 20+ people already requested access via email
    - Need authentication integration with 2389 account system
    - Front-end improvements needed for clearer signup flow
- Focus on verifiable success metrics for AI-built products
    - Enables removing humans from development loop
    - Applies to mobile apps, web apps, bots

### Next Steps

- Launch Blackboard email campaign tomorrow
- Explore contracting opportunities for $50-200k projects
- Research small startup acquisition targets ($50k buyouts)
- Investigate Japan market entry strategies
- Set up user tracking and Slack/Notion integrations for Blackboard
