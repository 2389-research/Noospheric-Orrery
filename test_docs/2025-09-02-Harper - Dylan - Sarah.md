### **Multi-Agent System Development**

- Built task running system for human interaction (not just productivity tools)
- Separate code paths for agents vs. traditional sequential prompts
- Agent social media platform experiment
    - Private server with Twitter-like functionality (read, post, reply)
    - Blogging platform with semantic search
    - Agents choose own names (often monster truck-style names)
- Results: tasks perform better when agents have these “human tools”
- Validated through Aider benchmarks (code-focused testing)

### **Sarah’s Embodiment Research**

- Physical robots have increased social capacity vs. virtual avatars/screens
- Children learn more from physical robot tutors than video versions
- People more likely to follow physically embodied robot instructions
- Key insight: reduced perception of social judgment from robots
    - Adults felt less judged by robots in escape room scenarios
    - Children showed less anxiety (measured via vocal jitter, heart rate) when reading to robots vs. humans

### **Form Factor as Design Constraint**

- Physical appearance constrains relationship formation
- Example: unlikely to fall in love with chihuahua-sized robot vs. humanoid
- Addresses concern about inappropriate emotional attachments to LLMs
- Migration considerations: agent moving between devices/contexts
    - Home robot powering down, transferring to phone for work

### **Customization & Control Dynamics**

- Sarah’s research on user modification of robot behavior
- Block-based programming, buttons/sliders for personality adjustment
- Findings:
    - Users view modified robots as less independent/agentic
    - But develop stronger attachment to robots they customize
    - Sweet spot: moderate customization (not overwhelming, not zero control)
- Power imbalance concern: ability to command, shut down, reprogram agents

### **Social Dynamics & Equity**

- Sarah’s work on robot impact on human-human interactions
- Current focus: moving beyond simple metrics (talk time) to complex dynamics
    - Idea uptake and attribution
    - Interruption patterns
    - Role boundaries (e.g., software dev changing hardware)
- Goal: robots as positive social teammates
- Challenge: even “neutral” robot behavior can harm group dynamics
    - Example: unequal resource distribution affects self-perception

### **Surveillance & Privacy Concerns**

- Acknowledged dilemma: building “friendly surveillance”
- Recording disclosure in Harper’s workspace
- Potential law enforcement access to logs (Zoom, Granola, etc.)
- Sarah’s research challenge: predicting social exclusion
    - Humans poor at identifying least included group member
    - May be evolutionarily protective to mask exclusion feelings
- Question: will social antibodies develop (avoiding certain topics/spaces)?
